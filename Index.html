About Studio 208

Built on precision.
Driven by vision.

Studio 208 is a specialist architectural rendering practice founded on a simple principle: every visualisation should be indistinguishable from the finished building. We work closely with architects, developers, and interior designers to ensure each image tells the right story.

From single-dwelling planning visuals to large-scale residential and commercial CGI suites, we bring the same level of craft and attention to every project — regardless of scale.
